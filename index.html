► SENTENCE DECONSTRUCTION ALGORITHM
1
Find the conjugated verb. Position 2 (main clause) · Position 1 (question/command) · END (after: weil, dass, wenn, obwohl, als, bevor, nachdem, damit, ob, falls, während, seitdem)
2
Who does the verb? → NOMINATIVE (der / die / das / die) — the Doer
3
What is directly verbed? → ACCUSATIVE — the Receiver. Masculine only changes: der→den, ein→einen
4
Is there a to/for recipient, dative preposition, or dative verb? → DATIVE — the Target. Use dem/der/dem/den+n
5
Is there possession or a genitive preposition? → GENITIVE — the Owner. des+s/der/des+s/der. Preps: wegen, trotz, während, (an)statt
